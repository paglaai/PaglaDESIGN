# The Mark

## Pagla Face

The Pagla Face is the primary symbol of PaglaAI.

It is a single-weight, black-ink only, minimal face with:

- Messy, genius hair
- Calm sunglasses
- A precise goatee

It is minimal but alive.

It keeps the duality of humanity and precision in a single mark.

## Construction

The mark is drawn once and reproduced infinitely.

Single weight.

No stroke variation.

No fill.

No gradients.

No effects.

Flat, black on paper.

## Rules

- The mark is black or white only.
- Never stretch, distort, or alter proportions.
- Add no colors, effects, shadows, or glow.
- Never crop or cut off the mark.
- Never outline or rotate the mark.
- Always use the whole mark.
- Use on clean paper.

Clearspace equals the height of the sunglasses.

The mark is used at a minimum of 24 pixels.

Below that size the mark is too small to preserve the identity.

---

# The Wordmark

The wordmark is not a logo.

It is not custom drawing.

It is the name set in PaglaAI Sans Light, precisely spaced.

## Rules

- Set in PaglaAI Sans Light 300.
- Optical tracking.
- No custom letters.
- No ligatures.
- The mark and type lock on a shared x-height.
- Never outline.
- Always set as live type.
- The wordmark is never weighted heavier than Regular.

The wordmark requires prescribed clear space.

It should not be used together with the Pagla Face.

---

# Color

PaglaAI identity is monochrome first.

Two colors—ink and paper—carry the brand.

An accent color is reserved for meaning only.

It reinforces hierarchy.

It never decorates.

It is never used as a background fill.

It appears only as a dot, an underline, or as focus.

The full semantic system, including roles and reserved meaning, is defined in `COLOR_SYSTEM.md`.

Reserve values are defined in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md`.

---

# Typography

PaglaAI Sans is the primary brand typeface.

PaglaAI Sans is a light, open, neutral family.

It shares the same humanist skeleton as trusted system families.

It is lighter, more open, and more precise. The design rationale and hierarchy are established in `TYPOGRAPHY.md`.

---

# Usage

## Dos

- Use a large open space.
- Keep the clearspace.
- Use black or white only.
- Use the mark flat.
- Always use the whole mark.
- Use it on clean paper.

## Don'ts

- Stretch or alter the mark in any way.
- Add colors to the mark.
- Add effects, shadows, or glow.
- Crop or cut off the mark.
- Use the mark over a busy image.
- Outline or rotate the mark.
- Add any unauthorized elements.

---

# Avatars and Icons

Avatars and app icons use the Pagla Face only.

The face is centered within the canvas.

The logo is never circled.

The logo is never used as an avatar.

On dark surfaces the mark appears white.

On light surfaces the mark appears black.

---

# Ecosystem

PaglaAI is one system across many surfaces.

Every product inherits the same mark, type, and usage rules.

The voice is shared.

Each product expresses a unique purpose within a shared identity.

---

# Prohibited

- Do not promote examples, styles, or marks of any outside system as PaglaAI identity.
- Do not claim the PaglaAI mark is derived from any other brand.
- Do not feature the mark more prominently than it needs to be.
- Do not reinvent the mark per project.
