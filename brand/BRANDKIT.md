# The Mark — Pagla Face

The Pagla Face is the primary symbol of PaglaAI.

A single-weight, black-ink-only, minimal face.

Messy genius hair. Calm sunglasses. A precise goatee.

Minimal but alive.

## Construction

- Drawn once, reproduced infinitely.
- Single weight. No fill. No gradients. No effects.
- Flat, black on paper.

## Rules

- Black or white only.
- Never stretch, distort, or alter proportions.
- No colors, effects, shadows, or glow.
- Never crop, outline, or rotate.
- Always use the whole mark.
- Clearspace equals the height of the sunglasses.
- Minimum size: 24 pixels.

---

# The Wordmark

The wordmark is the name set in PaglaAI Sans Light, precisely spaced.

- PaglaAI Sans Light 300.
- Optical tracking.
- No custom letters, no ligatures.
- Mark and type lock on a shared x-height.
- Never outline. Always live type.
- Never heavier than Regular.

The wordmark requires prescribed clear space.

It should not be used together with the Pagla Face.

# Usage

## Do

- Use a large open space.
- Keep the clearspace.
- Use black or white only.
- Use the mark flat.
- Always use the whole mark.
- Use it on clean paper.

## Don't

- Stretch or alter the mark.
- Add colors, effects, shadows, or glow.
- Crop, outline, or rotate the mark.
- Use the mark over a busy image.
- Add unauthorized elements.

## Partnerships

- Keep balanced spacing and clear hierarchy.
- Same height wordmarks.
- No colors added to the lockup.
- Approved by both brands before use.
