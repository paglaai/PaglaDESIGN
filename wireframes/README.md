# Wireframes

Exploratory wireframes and low-fidelity concepts.

**Purpose:**
Wireframes capture early thinking and explore multiple directions before committing to a design.

**Usage:**
- Use for user research, feedback gathering, and option exploration
- Not bound by design tokens (yet) — prioritize exploring the idea
- Include questions and notes about uncertainty
- Archive with dates when a direction is chosen

**When to migrate from wireframes/ to mockups/:**
1. Concept is validated through user research or team agreement
2. Design follows the design system (tokens, typography, color)
3. Ready for implementation or final review

**Relationship to mockups/:**
- `wireframes/` — exploratory, low-fidelity (thinking stage)
- `mockups/` — validated, high-fidelity, token-driven (commitment stage)
