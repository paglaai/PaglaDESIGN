# Cloudflare — Research Notes

> Non-canonical capture. These notes document what was studied and what was
> observed. They are not authority; the canonical documents remain the source
> of truth.

---

## What was studied

- Cloudflare documentation and developer surfaces; plain-language technical
  communication at scale.

## Observations

- Complex systems are explained in plain language, one step at a time.
- Documentation architecture is designed for scanning and searching.
- Trust comes from clarity, not from visual assertiveness.

## What applies to PaglaAI

- Approachable complexity (`../site/CONTENT_STRATEGY.md`).
- Searchable, scannable documentation (`../governance/DOCUMENTATION_STYLE.md`).
- Calm, confident technical voice.

## What does not apply

- Corporate visual treatments and busy homepage structures.
