# Apple — Research Notes

> Non-canonical capture. These notes document what was studied and what was
> observed. They are not authority; the canonical documents remain the source
> of truth.

---

## What was studied

- Apple marketing and product pages; whitespace discipline and hierarchy.

## Observations

- Whitespace is treated as a first-class design material.
- One idea per screen; hierarchy is unmissable.
- Typography and spacing carry emphasis before color.
- Product craft is communicated by restraint, not by decoration.

## What applies to PaglaAI

- Whitespace is content (`../PRINCIPLES.md`, #5).
- Generous spacing rhythm from the `space.*` tokens.
- Structure before ornament.

## What does not apply

- Heavy visual marketing production (photography-led pages).
- Slick product-film aesthetics and scroll-driven theatrics.
