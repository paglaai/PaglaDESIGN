# Anthropic — Research Notes

> Non-canonical capture. These notes document what was studied and what was
> observed. They are not authority; the canonical documents remain the source
> of truth.

---

## What was studied

- Anthropic product, brand, and documentation surfaces; tone and structure.

## Observations

- Calm, technical, trustworthy communication; no urgency marketing.
- Documentation-forward product design — docs are a first-class surface.
- Monochrome surfaces with restrained accent; subtle, purposeful motion.
- Clear hierarchy through type and whitespace rather than color blocks.

## What applies to PaglaAI

- Calm, technical tone (`../site/CONTENT_STRATEGY.md`).
- Documentation as a first-class deliverable (`../governance/DOCUMENTATION_STYLE.md`).
- Monochrome-first surfaces (`../design-system/COLOR_SYSTEM.md`).

## What does not apply

- Brand theatrics and decorative illustration systems.
