# OpenAI — Research Notes

> Non-canonical capture. These notes document what was studied and what was
> observed. They are not authority; the canonical documents in `brand/`,
> `design-system/`, and `../DESIGN.md` remain the source of truth.

---

## What was studied

- OpenAI product and communication pages (editorial, narrative style).
- Brand communication patterns rather than interface components.

## Observations

- Restrained, typographic presentation; the message carries the page.
- Narrative product storytelling — explain the *why* before the *what*.
- Minimal color; accent used for meaning, never decoration.
- Confident, plain language; no marketing inflation.
- A single clear next step per page, not competing calls to action.

## What applies to PaglaAI

- Editorial over decorative communication.
- One primary question per page.
- Accent-as-meaning discipline (matches `../design-system/COLOR_SYSTEM.md`).

## What does not apply

- Large gradient hero treatments and illustration-led marketing.
- Effect-driven page transitions.
