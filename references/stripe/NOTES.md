# Stripe — Research Notes

> Non-canonical capture. These notes document what was studied and what was
> observed. They are not authority; the canonical documents remain the source
> of truth.

---

## What was studied

- Stripe documentation and developer experience; progressive disclosure and
  developer empathy.

## Observations

- Docs teach progressively: concept before API, example before edge case.
- One idea per sentence; short paragraphs; concrete examples.
- Layout is generous; the reading column never fights the content.
- Developer experience is treated as a product in itself.

## What applies to PaglaAI

- Progressive disclosure (`../site/USER_JOURNEYS.md`).
- Bounded line measure (`font.maxWidth.body`).
- Documentation that teaches before it explains (`../site/CONTENT_STRATEGY.md`).

## What does not apply

- Chromatic gradient identity and illustration-heavy surfaces.
