# Vercel — Research Notes

> Non-canonical capture. These notes document what was studied and what was
> observed. They are not authority; the canonical documents remain the source
> of truth.

---

## What was studied

- Vercel marketing and documentation pages; typography-led developer branding.

## Observations

- Typography carries the brand; monochrome with a single functional accent.
- Fast, efficient layouts; marketing and docs feel like the same family.
- Plain, direct language aimed at developers.

## What applies to PaglaAI

- Typography as the primary interface (`../PRINCIPLES.md`, #4).
- One functional accent, never a background fill.
- A consistent family across marketing and documentation surfaces.

## What does not apply

- Marketing-driven homepages and effect-laden scroll presentation.
