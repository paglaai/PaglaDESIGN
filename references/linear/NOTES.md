# Linear — Research Notes

> Non-canonical capture. These notes document what was studied and what was
> observed. They are not authority; the canonical documents remain the source
> of truth.

---

## What was studied

- Linear's interface behavior: interaction crispness, focus discipline, and
  keyboard-first culture.

## Observations

- Micro-interactions are fast and carry meaning; nothing moves without reason.
- Focus states are explicit and never sacrificed for polish.
- Density serves the task; whitespace serves comprehension — both on purpose.
- State changes are communicated by more than color alone.

## What applies to PaglaAI

- Fast, purposeful motion (`../design-system/MOTION.md`).
- Visible, keyboard-accessible focus (`../design-system/ACCESSIBILITY.md`).
- Color is never the only carrier of state.

## What does not apply

- Very high information density and command-palette-first navigation.
